Grounded Answers demo
========================================================================
Fictional business: Seapoint Smile Dental, Cape Town. All data is synthetic.
Corpus: 34 documents (28 Markdown, 6 PDF), 163 chunks.
Embeddings: BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5. Reranker: Xenova/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2. Vector store: sqlite-vec.
Local models: live (downloaded), cache first
Generator and judge: replayed from fixtures/cassettes/ (hand-crafted, see the README).
