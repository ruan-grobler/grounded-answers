# 1 Before taking X-rays
Ask every patient who could be pregnant whether she is or may be pregnant. If the answer is yes or unsure, postpone any X-ray that is not urgent. For an urgent X-ray, the dentist decides, and the patient wears a lead apron.

# 2 Protection
Use a thyroid collar for every intra-oral X-ray. Staff stand behind the protective screen, or at least 2 metres away and out of the main beam, during every exposure. Never hold the film for a patient.

# 3 Dosimeter badges
Staff who take X-rays wear a dosimeter badge at work. The badges are sent away for reading every quarter.

# 4 How often to take X-rays
Adults at low risk of decay get bitewing X-rays every 2 years at their check-up. Patients at high risk of decay get them every year.

# 5 Equipment
Every X-ray unit is licensed and gets a quality control test once a year.
