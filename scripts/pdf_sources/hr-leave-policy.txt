# 1 Annual leave
Full-time staff get 15 working days of paid annual leave per year. Leave is counted in working days, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Apply on the leave form to the practice manager at least 2 weeks in advance. Leave of more than 10 consecutive working days needs the practice principal's approval.

# 2 December closure
Working days when the practice is closed over December are taken from annual leave. Keep enough annual leave for the closure.

# 3 Sick leave
Staff get 30 days of paid sick leave in each 36-month cycle. During the first 6 months of employment, staff earn 1 day of sick leave for every 26 days worked. A medical certificate is needed for an absence of more than 2 consecutive days, or for sick leave taken more than twice in 8 weeks.

# 4 Family responsibility leave
Staff get 3 days of paid family responsibility leave per year, for the birth of a child, a sick child, or the death of a close family member.

# 5 Maternity and parental leave
Staff may take 4 consecutive months of maternity leave, and the practice helps with the UIF claim. Parental leave for other parents is 10 consecutive days.

# 6 Reporting an absence
If you are sick, phone the practice manager before 07:30 on the day. A text message is not enough.
