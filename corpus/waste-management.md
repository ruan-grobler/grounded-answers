# Healthcare waste management

## Types of waste

Healthcare risk waste goes in red bags. Sharps go in yellow sharps containers. General waste goes in black bags. Amalgam waste goes in the sealed amalgam container.

## Sharps containers

Close and seal a sharps container when it is three-quarters full. Never overfill a container or push waste down into it. Label every sealed container with the date and the surgery.

## Collection

A licensed waste contractor collects healthcare risk waste every Tuesday morning. Until then, the waste is kept in the locked waste room. Keep every safe disposal certificate for 3 years.

## Amalgam

Check the amalgam separator in each surgery every month. Amalgam waste is collected twice a year.
