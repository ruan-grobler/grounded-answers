# Billing and payments

## When payment is due

Payment is due on the day of treatment. When the practice claims from a medical aid for the patient, the patient pays any shortfall within 30 days of the statement date.

## Payment methods

We accept debit and credit cards, instant EFT and QR code payments at reception. Cash is accepted up to R2,000 per visit. We do not accept cheques.

## Deposits for larger treatment

Treatment plans over R5,000 need a 50% deposit before treatment starts. The balance is due on the day the treatment is completed.

## Statements and overdue accounts

Statements are emailed on the first working day of each month. When an account is unpaid after 60 days, reception makes a reminder call and a R150 admin fee is added. After 90 days the account goes to the practice principal, who decides whether to hand it over for collection. Only discuss an account with the account holder.

## Refunds

Refunds are paid within 7 working days, to the original payment method, after the practice manager approves them.
