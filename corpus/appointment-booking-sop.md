# Appointment booking SOP

## Appointment lengths

Book these lengths in the practice management system:

- Check-up and examination: 30 minutes
- Scale and polish with the hygienist: 45 minutes
- Check-up with a scale and polish: 60 minutes
- New patient examination, including X-rays and a treatment plan: 60 minutes
- Filling: 45 minutes
- Extraction: 45 minutes
- Root canal treatment: 90 minutes
- Crown preparation: 90 minutes
- Crown fit: 45 minutes
- In-chair whitening: 90 minutes
- Child check-up: 30 minutes

## Booking rules

Book every appointment in the practice management system, never on paper. Confirm the patient's full name, date of birth and mobile number. New patients must complete registration before their first visit. Never double book a surgery. Emergencies go into the protected emergency slots, which are 10:30 to 11:00 and 14:30 to 15:00 every weekday.

## Confirmations and reminders

The system sends an automatic SMS reminder 48 hours before every appointment. New patients also get a second reminder on the morning of the appointment. If a patient replies CANCEL, reception calls them back the same day to rebook.

## Waiting list

Patients who want an earlier appointment go onto the waiting list. When a slot opens up, reception phones the waiting list in the order patients were added.

## When the dentist is running late

If a dentist is running more than 15 minutes late, reception tells the waiting patients and offers to rebook anyone who cannot wait.
