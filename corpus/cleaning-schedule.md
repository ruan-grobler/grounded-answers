# Surgery cleaning schedule

## Between patients

Disinfect all touched surfaces as set out in the infection control policy, and replace the barrier covers on the chair controls, light handles and keyboard.

## End of day

Flush the suction lines with suction cleaner, clean the spittoon, empty the waste bins and mop the floor in every surgery.

## Weekly

Clean the suction filters and treat the dental unit waterlines with the waterline cleaner every Friday after the last patient.

## Monthly

Deep clean every surgery on the last Saturday of the month, after the practice closes.
