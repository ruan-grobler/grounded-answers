# Opening hours and public holidays

## Regular hours

The practice is open Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 17:00 and on Saturday from 08:00 to 12:00. We are closed on Sundays. Every appointment must finish by closing time, so check the appointment length before you offer the last slot of the day.

## Reception hours

Reception answers the phone from 07:45 to 17:00 on weekdays and from 07:45 to 12:00 on Saturdays. Outside these hours calls go to voicemail, and the message gives the after-hours emergency number.

## Public holidays

The practice is closed on all South African public holidays. The remaining public holidays in 2026 are the Day of Reconciliation on Wednesday 16 December, Christmas Day on Friday 25 December and the Day of Goodwill on Saturday 26 December. New Year's Day 2027 is on Friday 1 January.

## December closure

The practice closes for the year on Thursday 24 December 2026 and reopens on Monday 4 January 2027. The after-hours emergency line stays on during the closure, using the normal on-call rota.
