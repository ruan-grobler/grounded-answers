# Patient records and retention

## What a patient record contains

A patient record contains the registration form, the medical history, clinical notes, charts, X-rays and scans, treatment plans and quotes, consent forms, and correspondence with medical aids and specialists.

## Retention periods

Keep adult patient records for at least 6 years after the last visit. Keep a child's records until the patient's 21st birthday or for 6 years after the last visit, whichever is later. X-rays are kept for as long as the record they belong to. Sterilisation logs are kept for 5 years and waste disposal certificates for 3 years. Staff files are kept for 3 years after employment ends.

## Storage

Records are kept electronically in the practice management system. Paper documents are scanned on the day, checked against the original, and the paper is shredded only after the scan has been checked.

## Destruction

Records past their retention period are destroyed by cross-cut shredding or certified digital deletion. The Information Officer approves every destruction, and it is recorded in the destruction register.
