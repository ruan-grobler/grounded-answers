# Treatment consent policy

## When written consent is needed

Written informed consent is needed for extractions, root canal treatment, crowns and bridges, whitening, any treatment under sedation, and any treatment that costs more than R3,000. For check-ups, cleaning and X-rays, verbal consent recorded in the clinical notes is enough.

## What the dentist explains

Before asking for consent, the dentist explains the diagnosis, the proposed treatment, the alternatives including no treatment, the risks, and the cost.

## Patients under 18

For every patient under 18, the practice asks a parent or legal guardian to sign the consent form.

## Withdrawing consent

A patient may withdraw consent at any time, including during treatment. The dentist records the withdrawal in the clinical notes.
