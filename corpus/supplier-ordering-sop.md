# Stock ordering SOP

## Ordering day

The senior dental assistant counts stock on Wednesday and places orders every Thursday.

## Approval limits

The senior dental assistant approves orders up to R5,000. Orders over R5,000 need the practice manager's approval, and orders over R20,000 need the practice principal's approval.

## Receiving deliveries

Check every delivery against the order, check expiry dates, store the stock and sign the delivery note.

## Changing supplier bank details

Never change a supplier's bank details because of an email. Call the supplier on the phone number already on file to confirm, and get the practice manager's approval before any change.
