# Practice overview

## About the practice

Seapoint Smile Dental is a general dental practice in Sea Point, Cape Town. It has three surgeries and one hygiene room on the ground floor of Unit 4, 12 Example Road, Sea Point, Cape Town, 8005. The practice principal is Dr Priya Naidoo. We see adults and children for general, preventive and cosmetic dentistry.

## Contact details

The main line is +27 00 000 0000 and the reception email is reception@seapointsmile.example.com. The after-hours emergency line is +27 00 000 0999. Staff work contacts are in the staff directory.

## Parking and access

There are four patient parking bays behind the building, with the entrance from the service lane. Street parking on Example Road is metered. The entrance is step-free. Surgery 2 and the patient bathroom are wheelchair accessible, so book patients who use a wheelchair into surgery 2.

## Surgeries and rooms

Surgery 1 is Dr Naidoo's room. Surgeries 2 and 3 are used by the associate dentists. The hygiene room is next to reception. The sterilisation room is behind reception, and the X-ray room with the panoramic unit is next to surgery 3.

## Referrals

Patients who need a specialist, for example an oral surgeon or a periodontist, get a referral letter from the treating dentist within 2 working days. Reception sends the letter and the relevant X-rays to the specialist only after the patient has agreed in writing.
