# Complaints handling

## Receiving a complaint

Any staff member can receive a complaint, in person, by phone or in writing. Record it in the complaints register on the same day.

## Response times

Acknowledge every complaint within 2 working days. Send the patient a full response within 15 working days.

## Escalation

Clinical complaints go to the treating dentist and the practice principal. Complaints about fees go to the practice manager. If the patient is not satisfied with the practice's response, the practice principal tells them how to complain to the professional regulator.

## Learning from complaints

Complaints are reviewed, without patient names, at the monthly staff meeting.
