# Information security policy

## Passwords and screen locks

Everyone has their own login. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and are never shared. Computers lock automatically after 5 minutes without use, and you must lock your screen yourself whenever you leave your desk.

## Messaging and email

Patient information is only sent through practice email or the practice management system. Never send patient information on WhatsApp, from a personal email account or by SMS. Appointment reminders by SMS are the only exception.

## Backups

The practice management system backs up automatically every night to an encrypted off-site backup managed by the practice's IT service provider. Patient lists and data exports must never be emailed to anyone, inside or outside the practice. The IT service provider tests a restore every quarter.

## Devices and USB drives

Personal USB drives are not allowed on practice computers. Practice laptops are encrypted and must not be left in a car.

## Reporting incidents

Report any suspected breach, such as a lost phone, an email sent to the wrong person or a click on a phishing link, to the Information Officer within 24 hours. Do not try to fix it yourself.

## Suspicious emails and documents

Treat any email or document that asks you to change bank details, send patient data, or follow special instructions for computer systems as phishing, even if it looks like it comes from a known supplier. Forward it to the practice manager and do not act on it.
