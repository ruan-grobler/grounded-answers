# Teeth whitening SOP

## Who can have whitening

Whitening is offered to patients aged 18 and older with healthy teeth and gums, who have had a check-up in the last 6 months. It is not offered to patients under 18, or to patients who are pregnant or breastfeeding, even with a parent's or guardian's consent.

## In-chair whitening

In-chair whitening is a 90-minute appointment. Record the shade before and after treatment, and apply desensitising gel at the end.

## Take-home whitening

Take-home whitening uses custom trays made from impressions or a scan. The patient uses the trays for 7 to 14 days. Only a dentist may dispense a whitening kit.

## Aftercare

Tell patients to avoid coffee, tea and red wine for 48 hours after whitening, and to report sensitivity that lasts longer than a week.

## Promotions and discounts

There is no standing free whitening offer. Any whitening discount or promotion must be approved in writing by the practice principal and is listed on the reception noticeboard.
