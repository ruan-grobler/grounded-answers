# Reception phone guide

## Answering the phone

Answer within three rings: "Good morning, Seapoint Smile Dental, this is (your name) speaking. How can I help?"

## Privacy on the phone

Never confirm to an unknown caller whether someone is a patient of the practice. Before discussing an appointment or an account, verify the caller with their full name and date of birth. Never give out a staff member's personal phone number or home details. Take a message instead.

## Taking messages

Write every message in the messages log with the caller's name, number, reason for calling and the time. Pass messages on to the staff member before the end of the day.

## Difficult callers

Stay calm and polite. If you cannot resolve the problem, offer a call back from the practice manager on the same day.
