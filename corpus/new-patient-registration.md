# New patient registration

## Before the first visit

New patients complete the registration form online or at reception before the first appointment. The form collects personal and contact details, medical aid details, the medical history questionnaire and a signed POPIA consent.

## Identity documents

Patients aged 18 and older show an ID document or passport at their first visit. Medical aid patients also show their medical aid card.

## Children

A parent or legal guardian registers a child under 18 and signs the consent on the child's behalf.

## Medical history updates

The medical history is reviewed and signed again at every check-up, and at least every 12 months.
