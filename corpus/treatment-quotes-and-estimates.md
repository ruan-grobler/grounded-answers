# Treatment quotes and estimates

## When a written quote is needed

Any treatment plan over R3,000 gets a written quote before treatment starts. Smaller treatment can be quoted verbally, and the dentist notes the verbal quote in the patient file.

## How long a quote is valid

A quote is valid for 90 days from the date it was issued. After 90 days the dentist must re-quote at the current fees.

## What a quote includes

A quote lists each procedure with its code and fee, the expected number of visits, any laboratory fees, and an estimate of medical aid cover that is clearly marked as an estimate.

## Accepting a quote

The patient signs the quote and the treatment consent form, and reception scans both into the patient file. The deposit rules in the billing policy apply.
