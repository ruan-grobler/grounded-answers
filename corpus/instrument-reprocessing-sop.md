# Instrument reprocessing SOP

## Zones in the sterilisation room

Instruments move in one direction, from dirty to clean: receiving, cleaning, inspection and packaging, sterilisation, then storage. Never carry clean or sterile items back through the dirty zone.

## Cleaning

Wear heavy-duty gloves. Put instruments in the ultrasonic cleaner for 10 minutes, or run them through the washer disinfector. Only scrub by hand under water, to avoid splashes.

## Inspection and packaging

Inspect every instrument under the magnifying lamp for debris and damage. Pack instruments in pouches with a chemical indicator. Label every pouch with the date, the autoclave cycle number and the initials of the person who packed it.

## Storage and shelf life

Store sterile pouches in closed drawers. An intact pouch can be used for up to 6 months. Re-sterilise any pouch that is torn, wet or has been dropped on the floor.

## Single-use items

Never reprocess single-use items such as needles, saliva ejectors and disposable prophy angles. They go straight into the correct waste stream after use.
