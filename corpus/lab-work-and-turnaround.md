# Laboratory work and turnaround times

## Sending a case

Every case goes to the dental laboratory with a completed lab form, the impressions or scan, and the shade. Log the case in the lab book with the date it was sent.

## Turnaround times

Count turnaround in working days from the day after the case is sent. Crowns and bridges take 7 working days. Dentures take 10 working days. Night guards and whitening trays take 5 working days.

## Booking the fit appointment

Book the fit appointment for the first working day after the lab's due date, so there is time to check the work before the patient arrives.

## Checking returned work

Check every returned case against the lab form. If the shade or fit is wrong, return it to the laboratory on the same day and tell the treating dentist.
