# Cancellations and no-shows

## Notice required

Patients must give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment. They can give notice by phone, by replying to the SMS reminder, or by email to reception.

## Late cancellation fee

A cancellation with less than 24 hours' notice is charged a R350 late cancellation fee. For appointments of 90 minutes or longer, such as root canal treatment, crown preparations and in-chair whitening, the late cancellation fee is R700.

## No-show fee

A patient who does not arrive and gives no notice is charged a no-show fee of R500, or R900 for appointments of 90 minutes or longer. Medical aids do not pay cancellation or no-show fees.

## When fees are waived

The practice manager may waive a late cancellation or no-show fee once per patient in a calendar year, and always waives it for a documented medical emergency or a bereavement. Record the reason in the patient file.

## Repeat no-shows

After three no-shows in 12 months, the patient must pay a R500 booking deposit before any new appointment is confirmed. The deposit is used against the treatment on the day.
