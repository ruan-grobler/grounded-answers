# Medical aid claims

## How we claim

Reception submits claims electronically on the day of treatment for patients who gave their medical aid details at registration. The patient stays responsible for the account, even when we claim for them.

## Our fees and scheme rates

The practice charges its own fees from the fee schedule. Medical schemes pay at their own rate, which can be lower than our fee. The difference is the shortfall, and the patient pays it.

## Pre-authorisation

Crowns, bridges, dentures and any treatment under sedation need pre-authorisation from the medical scheme before treatment. Reception requests it at least 10 working days before the appointment and tells the patient straight away if the scheme refuses.

## Rejected claims

If a scheme rejects a claim, reception tells the patient within 5 working days. The full amount then becomes payable by the patient within 30 days.

## Checking benefits

Reception can check a patient's available benefits with the scheme on request, but must tell the patient that the scheme does not guarantee payment until the claim is processed.
