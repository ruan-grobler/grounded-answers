# Code of conduct

## Dress code

Clinical staff wear practice scrubs and closed shoes, with long hair tied back. Nails are kept short and unpolished, with no gel or acrylic nails, for infection control. Reception staff wear the practice shirt.

## Punctuality

Arrive at least 15 minutes before your first appointment of the day, so the surgery is ready on time.

## Gifts from patients and suppliers

Declare any gift worth more than R500 to the practice manager, who records it in the gifts register. Never accept cash from a patient or a supplier.

## Social media

Never post photos of patients, X-rays or a surgery with a patient in it. Do not post about patients, even without names. Only the practice manager posts on the practice's own social media accounts.

## Confidentiality

Everything you learn about patients and colleagues at work is confidential, during and after your employment.
