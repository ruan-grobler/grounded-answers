# Remuneration and overtime

## Pay day

Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month. If the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday, salaries are paid on the last working day before it. Payslips are emailed to your work email address.

## Overtime

Overtime must be approved in advance by the practice manager. It is paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate, or 2 times the normal rate on Sundays and public holidays.

## Saturday rota

Saturday mornings are worked on a rota. For staff on the Saturday rota, the Saturday shift is part of normal working hours. Staff who are not on the rota and are asked to work a Saturday are paid overtime.

## Salary information is confidential

Individual salaries are confidential. Only the practice principal, the practice manager and the payroll provider see them. Do not ask about or share anyone else's pay.

## Expense claims

Submit expense claims with the receipts within 30 days of the expense. The practice manager approves all claims.
