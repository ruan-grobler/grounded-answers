# Infection control policy

## Responsibilities

The infection control lead is the senior dental assistant. The infection control lead checks compliance every month, trains new staff at induction and all staff once a year, and keeps the infection control file up to date.

## Hand hygiene

Clean your hands before and after every patient and every time you remove gloves, with alcohol hand rub or soap and water.

## Personal protective equipment

Wear gloves, a mask and eye protection for every patient. Wear a gown for procedures that create aerosols. Change your mask and gloves between patients.

## Needle-stick and sharps injuries

Let the wound bleed freely and wash it with soap and running water. Do not scrub or suck the wound. Report the injury to the practice manager within 1 hour and complete an incident form. The treating dentist assesses the source patient's risk. If the source patient is high risk or unknown, the staff member must be assessed for post-exposure prophylaxis at the nearest emergency unit within 2 hours.

## Surface disinfection

Between patients, wipe every surface that was touched with an approved disinfectant wipe and leave it wet for a contact time of 1 minute.
