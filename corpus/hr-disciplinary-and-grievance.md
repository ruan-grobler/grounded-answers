# Disciplinary and grievance procedure

## Raising a grievance

Speak to your line manager first. If the grievance is not resolved within 5 working days, put it in writing to the practice manager. If the grievance is about the practice manager, write to the practice principal instead. A meeting is held within 10 working days of the written grievance.

## Disciplinary steps

The steps are a verbal warning, a written warning, a final written warning and then a disciplinary hearing. A verbal warning stays on file for 6 months, and a written or final written warning for 12 months.

## Serious misconduct

Serious misconduct includes theft, sharing patient information without permission, working under the influence of alcohol or drugs, and falsifying sterilisation logs. Serious misconduct can lead to dismissal after a hearing.

## Representation

A staff member may bring a colleague to any disciplinary hearing or grievance meeting.
