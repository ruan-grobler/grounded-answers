# Children's dentistry

## First visit

We recommend a child's first visit by their first birthday, or within 6 months of the first tooth coming through.

## Parent or guardian present

A parent or legal guardian must come with every patient under 18, or give written permission for another adult to bring the child.

## Fissure sealants and fluoride

Fissure sealants are offered on the permanent molars from about age 6. Children at high risk of decay get fluoride varnish every 6 months.

## Appointment times for children

Book children under 12 before 11:00 where possible, because young children cope better in the morning.
